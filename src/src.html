Breadcrumbs

Photos carry hidden details: the exact spot where they were taken, the time and your phone model. Put a few photos together and a stranger can work out where you live. Check what yours give away, then make clean copies, before you post or send them.

3. Make clean copies

Breadcrumbs redraws each photo as a new JPEG with no hidden details, then reads the new file back to prove nothing is left. Your originals are not changed.

What Breadcrumbs does not catch

  • Things you can see in the picture: house numbers, street signs, school logos, a view from your window.
  • Breadcrumbs reads hidden details from JPEG, PNG, WebP and HEIC. It can't redraw HEIC or RAW files into clean copies, so export those as JPEG first.
  • On iPhone, the browser's photo picker may convert photos and drop their location before Breadcrumbs sees them. The same photo sent as an email attachment can still carry it. Check the files you will actually send.
  • Clean copies use the standard sRGB colors, so photos from wide-color cameras may look slightly less vivid. Privacy browsers that block canvas (Tor, Firefox with fingerprinting resistance) may produce blank copies.
  • Some apps remove location when you post. Many ways of sharing keep it: email attachments, "send as file" in chat apps, cloud links, and some marketplaces and forums.
